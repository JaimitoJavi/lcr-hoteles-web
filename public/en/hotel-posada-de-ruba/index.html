La Posada de Ruba

Located in the center of Biescas, this accommodation with character and tradition is designed for those who seek practicality. Its combination of history, functionality, freedom and comfort will make you experience the Pyrenees in its purest form at any time of the year. Perfect for exploring the natural environment, practicing sports or resting surrounded by mountains.

La Cuchara de Ruba Restaurant

On the ground floor of the hotel, you will find La Cuchara de Ruba, a restaurant by Chef Diego Herrero. You can taste traditional Alto Aragonese cuisine with avant-garde touches in a menu that changes almost daily.

Location

Biescas, your gateway to the Pyrenees

In the heart of Biescas, our location connects you with the best of the Aragonese Pyrenees. Experience the nature of the Ordesa-Viñamala Reserve and reach the ski resorts of Formigal, Panticosa, Candanchú or Astún in a few minutes, as well as the historic town of Jaca.

RENFE Station Sabiñánigo, 15 km away.RENFE Jaca Station, 30 km away.
