Destination

Explore the nature of Huesca

Biescas, in the heart of the Aragonese Pyrenees, is the perfect destination to unwind and be surprised by its mountain village charm. Here, nature and authenticity come together to offer you much more than a place to sleep: a unique experience where you can create unforgettable moments.

Activities

Pyrenees 4 seasons

In the privileged Pyrenees of Huesca, the best vacation plans you can imagine are waiting for you, all year round. Share intense ski and snow days on the slopes of Formigal and Panticosa; experience thrilling journeys through the Ordesa National Park or dare to descend down the largest zip line in Europe. We are the premiere destination for family tourism with children, romantic getaways, or intrepid adventures.
