Live the magic of the Pyrenees

Our apartments are located in Biescas, a charming village with access to the mountains and valleys of the Aragonese Pyrenees. Located in a privileged enclave, next to the Ordesa-Viñamala Reserve, and just a stone’s throw away from the ski resorts of Formigal and Panticosa. The perfect combination of nature, culture and sports for a unique experience.

RENFE station Sabiñánigo, 15 km away. RENFE Jaca Station, 30 km away.
