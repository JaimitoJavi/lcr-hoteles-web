Design your perfect wedding!

The peaceful natural surroundings of the Hotel Tierra de Biescas are the ideal setting for designing a wedding tailored to your dreams.

If you choose Biescas to celebrate your religious wedding, our town has the beautiful churches of San Salvador and San Pedro. And, just a 10-minute drive away, one of the most special spaces in the Tena Valley is offered to you: the intimate and legendary hermitage of Santa Elena. If you have opted for a civil wedding, at the Hotel Tierra de Biescas we offer you extensive facilities and more than 3,000 m2 of garden to share your eternal “I do”. The peaceful natural environment of the Hotel is the ideal setting to design a wedding tailored to your dreams. Our Santa Elena Hall is prepared to organize your banquet with recognized Professional Service and ‘Haute Cuisine’ Menu.

EVENT ORGANISATION DEPARTMENT

Experience and commitment

The organisation of a wedding event requires special sensitivity in each of the details of your unique wedding day. So, in addition to working with the ‘heart’, we plan each event with meticulous demand to ensure that your celebration goes with impeccable coordination, without leaving room for improvisation.

We wish to convey to the newlyweds the tranquility and confidence that their wedding day will unfold just as they have dreamed: happily enjoying every moment!

High Cuisine Banquets

The kitchen team at La Bastille always works with market products, fresh meat and fish, chosen daily by the chef. Their varied menu allows the couple to freely select their preferences and, if they wish, create their own menu. As a courtesy detail, we gift the couple the personalization service of their banquet menus, where they can also include their particular dedication.

The requirements of the children audience and diners who are vegetarians, vegans, have allergies or food intolerance are anticipated by our Catering Service.

AFTER THE BANQUET

Dance, party, open bar and late-night snack

Music, dance, show and open bar, supper and fun for all audiences is the star and inseparable combination in the festive chapter of our banquets.

The animation is guaranteed with ‘à la carte’ musical programming, with live performances, orchestra, karaoke and magic. And as the party progresses, comes the time for the popular Aragonese Second Supper with “Tierra” style breadcrumbs with Graus sausage and chorizo, accompanied by cider and a good winery.
