Gastronomy

La Lifara Restaurant

At the ‘La Lifara’ Restaurant-Terrace, we offer Lunch and Dinner service every day. We have a wide and varied menu of dishes inspired by traditional Mediterranean cuisine.

Our uniqueness? It is allowed to share all the rations. We want to preserve the sense of social celebration that we inherited from our Aragonese identity, alifara (“feast or snack usually given on the occasion of a purchase one makes of something”) We are waiting for you!

Opening hours

Dinners

Every day, Monday to Sunday, from 8:00 p.m. to 10:30 p.m.

Lunches

Saturdays, Sundays, long weekends, and July and August from 1:30 p.m. to 3:30 p.m.

Buffet breakfast, at your own pace!

Are you into hearty, indulgent, or light breakfasts? Every morning at our Buffet you can choose a different breakfast. Combine your way a wide variety of sweet and savory, hot and cold, natural and prepared propositions… And remember, whether you’re the first or the last one to arrive, there will always be plenty to accompany your favorite breakfast. Feel free to have seconds!

Opening hours

7:30 a.m. to 10:30 a.m.

What does our "La Lifara" buffet include

  • Coffee, infusions, chocolate and a variety of milks (whole, skimmed, lactose-free and soy) Pastries and a variety of baked goods: puff pastry croissants, muffins, carrot cake, cookies and sugar-free cereal biscuits.
  • Bread menu: loaf, walnut and raisin, sourdough, multi-grain, and sandwich bread. Bacon, sausages and assorted cold cuts (ham, prosciutto, cheese and chorizo) Scrambled eggs, fried eggs, boiled eggs and homemade Spanish omelette.
  • Seasonal fruits, prepared fruit, orange juice, multivitamin juice and tomato juice. Natural yogurt, flavored yogurt, skimmed yogurt and liquid yogurt.
  • Extras: jams, cereals, muesli, nuts, crushed natural tomatoes, honey, butter and oils from Aragon.
