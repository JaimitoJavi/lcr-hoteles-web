leisure and work

Corporate events

In Biescas, work and enjoy! Because each event requires a distinctive atmosphere, the Tierra de Biescas Hotel Complex has versatile rooms adaptable to the requirements of congresses, business events and training sessions, as well as product presentations, team building and gala dinners.

The location, altitude and privileged orientation of Biescas in the Pyrenees of Huesca allow for planning work days with alternative activities: outdoor teamwork, sports, multi-adventure, recreational and thematic.

everything you need

Facilities and services

Our facilities are equipped with comprehensive audiovisual equipment and are conditioned to meet the demands of various professional meetings:

  • Santa Elena Hall: designed to enable independent rooms or provide a large multi-purpose room of 300 m², with optimal capacity for 150 people.
  • Outdoor terraces of the Pyrenees: facing north and south, prepared for professional events or informal meetings. With their own and personalized catering and buffet services.
  • The 3,000 sqm of our gardens expand the possibilities of enabling unique spaces, especially in spring and summer. Hotel Tierra de Biescas also offers the possibility to reserve the outdoor pool and the SPA-Wellness space with a heated recreational pool.
