Hotel Tierra de Biescas

Four-star hotel complex in the village of Biescas. We offer you the best vacation plans in the Pyrenees: from the extensive slopes of Formigal and Panticosa to the magnificent viewpoints of the Ordesa National Park. A meeting place for tourism with children, romantic getaways, intrepid adventures, sports meetings or charming weddings, here, in the Valle de Tena, we will discover the secrets of the magic of Huesca.

High-altitude weddings

Experience a dream wedding at our hotel and say “I do” in an unparalleled natural setting. Our versatile spaces and the best personalized services will create a magical moment you will never forget.

Sports Stages

We host sports stages, offering complete sports facilities, an optimal altitude and a privileged environment in the Pyrenees of Huesca. With 20 years of experience, we accommodate athletes and teams looking for quality and efficiency in their preparation.

Company events

In the land of Biescas there is also room for work. Our hotel has adaptable multi-purpose rooms, perfect for organizing different corporate events, conferences, product presentations and teambuilding.

Location

Pso. del Canal, s/n 22630 Biescas, Huesca, Spain

The Pyrenees in the palm of your hand

We are located in the village of Biescas, in the surroundings of the Ordesa-Viñamala Biosphere Reserve. Just a step away from the Formigal and Panticosa stations, its privileged location facilitates direct access to Jaca and the Candanchú and Astún slopes, in the Tena Valley.
