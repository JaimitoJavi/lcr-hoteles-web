Live the magic of the Pyrenees

In Biescas, a charming town in the Aragonese Pyrenees, our apartments are located, surrounded by the Ordesa-Viñamala Nature Reserve and near the Formigal and Panticosa ski resorts. From here, it is easy to reach Jaca and the slopes of Candanchú and Astún in the Aragon Valley. A perfect destination to combine nature, culture, and sports.

RENFE station Sabiñánigo, 15 km away. RENFE Jaca Station, 30 km away.

Zaragoza Airport, 148 km away.
