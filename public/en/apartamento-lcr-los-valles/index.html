LCR Los Valles Apartment

The Los Valles apartment, recently renovated, offers natural light and exterior views from all its rooms, thanks to its privileged orientation. Its modern equipment and warm interior design are designed for families who want to enjoy, at their own pace, a getaway to the Pyrenees with all the comforts. Located in the quiet Los Valles urbanization, surrounded by spacious wooded areas, you will have free access to its magnificent pool and a spectacular barbecue area with a terrace, perfect for relaxing or sharing unforgettable moments.

Biescas, your ideal base in the Pyrenees

We are located in Biescas, at the foothills of the Aragonese Pyrenees. Located next to the Ordesa-Viñamala Biosphere Reserve and the Formigal and Panticosa ski resorts, we offer direct access to Jaca and the white slopes of Candanchú and Astún. Nature, culture, and sports come together to give you an unforgettable experience.

RENFE Sabiñánigo Station, 15 km away.RENFE Jaca Station, 30 km away.
